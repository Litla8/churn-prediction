# Top drivers of churn (SHAP)

1. **Higher NumOfProducts lowers predicted churn risk** (average impact 12.9 probability points)
2. **Higher Age raises predicted churn risk** (average impact 12.8 probability points)
3. **Higher IsActiveMember lowers predicted churn risk** (average impact 6.0 probability points)
4. **Higher Balance raises predicted churn risk** (average impact 3.1 probability points)
5. **Gender = Female raises predicted churn risk** (average impact 2.9 probability points)

Direction is the overall tendency; some relationships are not monotonic (for example, risk rises with age and then falls in the oldest group). These are associations in the data, not proven causes.
